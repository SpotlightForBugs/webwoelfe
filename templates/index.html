About the Game

Welcome to Werewolf, a game of deception, strategy, and survival. In this deadly village, werewolves hide among innocent villagers, hunting at night while the village seeks to identify and eliminate the threat during the day.

Each player is assigned a role:

  • 🐺 Werewolf - Your goal is to eliminate the villagers
  • 👁️ Seer - You can reveal the true identity of one player each night
  • 🧙‍♀️ Witch - You possess potions to heal or kill
  • 💘 Cupid - You link two players in love who will share the same fate
  • 🏹 Hunter - You take someone with you when you die
  • 👨‍👩‍👧‍👦 Villager - Your goal is to identify and eliminate the werewolves

Will you survive the night, or succumb to the werewolves' hunger?
